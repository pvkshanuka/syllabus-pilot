Course Description:
This course introduces fundamental concepts of computer programming, algorithms, data structures, and computational thinking using Python. Topics include control flow, modular design, recursion, object-oriented concepts, and basic sorting and searching algorithms.

Course Topics & Schedule:
- Week 1 (Sep 07 - Sep 11): Variables, Primitive Types, and Control Flow
- Week 2 (Sep 14 - Sep 18): Functions, Scope, and Modular Design
- Week 3 (Sep 21 - Sep 25): Strings, Lists, and Dictionaries
- Week 4 (Sep 28 - Oct 02): File I/O, Error Handling, and Exceptions
- Week 5 (Oct 05 - Oct 09): Recursion and Algorithmic Complexity (Big O)
- Week 6 (Oct 12 - Oct 16): Midterm 1 Review and Exam
- Week 7 (Oct 19 - Oct 23): Object-Oriented Programming (Classes, Inheritance)
- Week 8 (Oct 26 - Oct 30): Data Structures I (Stacks, Queues, Linked Lists)
- Week 9 (Nov 02 - Nov 06): Data Structures II (Trees, Hash Tables)
- Week 10 (Nov 09 - Nov 13): Searching and Sorting Algorithms
- Week 11 (Nov 16 - Nov 20): Midterm 2 Review and Exam
- Week 12 (Nov 23 - Nov 27): Graph Basics & BFS/DFS
- Week 13 (Nov 30 - Dec 04): Capstone Project Work & Code Reviews
- Week 14 (Dec 07 - Dec 11): Final Project Presentations & Course Review
- Week 15 (Dec 14 - Dec 18): Final Exam Week
